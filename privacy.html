Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2026

At Mithaq Luxe, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our discerning clientele. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including information about your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some of the cookies that are installed on your device. Additionally, when you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through the Site, we collect certain information from you, including your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information (including credit card numbers or alternative payment gateways), email address, and phone number.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the order information that we collect generally to fulfill any orders placed through the Site (including processing your payment information, arranging for shipping, and providing you with invoices and/or order confirmations). Additionally, we use this information to:

  • Communicate with you and provide bespoke customer support.
  • Screen our orders for potential risk or fraud.
  • Provide you with information or advertising relating to our exclusive collections, events, or product launches, in line with the preferences you have shared with us.

3. Sharing Your Personal Information

We share your Personal Information with trusted third parties to help us use your Personal Information, as described above. For example, we use Shopify/WooCommerce to power our online boutique, and Stripe/PayPal to process secure payments. We also use Google Analytics to help us understand how our customers navigate the boutique.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through the Site, we will maintain your Order Information for our records unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR for European residents), you have the right to access personal information we hold about you and to ask that your personal information be corrected, updated, or deleted. If you would like to exercise this right, please contact us through the contact information below.

6. Contact Us

For more information about our privacy practices, or if you have questions, please contact us by e-mail at concierge@mithaqluxe.com.
