Heritage Refined

The curator’s selection: a symphony of rare and precious essences.

Mithaq is born from an unwavering passion to procure the world’s most unique and coveted raw ingredients. Our process honors the ancient art of selection, gathering rare and precious elements to craft truly bespoke scent experiences.

Each essential element is meticulously vetted, then aged in a singular, temperature-controlled environment. This curation ensures the volatile heart of the oil remains pristine, delivering a complex, long-wearing aura that evolves on the skin.
